1. The platform at a glance

Service Marketplace connects local service vendors — AC repair, plumbing, electrical work, cleaning, packers & movers and more — with customers who need them. Vendors pay a yearly subscription to be listed in the areas and services they choose. Customers browse for free and contact vendors directly by phone call or WhatsApp.

Vendors join mainly through salesmen, who visit businesses, sign them up and collect payment; vendors can also sign up themselves. An admin team manages the catalogue, approves accounts and documents, checks payments and moderates content.

Web panels (Laravel + Filament), dark theme, same login.
Server (API)
Laravel 12 with a MySQL/MariaDB database. All three apps talk to it; it owns every rule — prices, quotas, dates and permissions are always worked out on the server, never trusted from an app.

2. How it works — the main journeys

2.1 A salesman signs up a vendor (main channel)

Add vendor details→Upload KYC documents→Choose plan, services, areas→Take payment→Admin verifies KYC→Vendor live
  1. The salesman enters the business (name, owner, phone, address, GPS location, shop photo) in the salesman app.
  2. They upload the vendor's KYC documents (for example Aadhaar and PAN — the admin decides which are compulsory).
  3. They pick a plan (Silver / Gold / Platinum), then the categories, services and zones within the plan's limits.
  4. Payment: cash (the salesman collects it; an admin later confirms it in Cash Reconciliation) or an online payment link sent by SMS (Razorpay). A free trial can also be granted.
  5. The vendor is emailed a "set your password" link to sign in to the vendor app.
  6. The vendor becomes visible to customers once the subscription is paid and an admin has verified every compulsory KYC document and approved the account.
  7. The salesman earns a commission on the sale, which the admin pays out from Commission & Payouts.

2.2 A vendor signs up by themselves

Register (name, email, phone)→Set password from email link→Upload KYC→Admin approves→Choose plan & pay→Live

Registration does not ask for a password: the app shows "Check your email", and the account's first password is set from the emailed link. Until an admin approves the account the app shows "Your account is in verification".

2.3 A customer finds and contacts a vendor

Choose area→Pick a service→See matching vendors→Vendor page→Call / WhatsApp→Review
  1. The customer sets their location: by picking an area from a list (default) or, if the admin turns it on, on a map / GPS / place search.
  2. The server works out the customer's zone and lists vendors whose live subscription covers that zone and the chosen service.
  3. Results are ordered by plan tier first (Platinum before Gold before Silver), then by the sort the customer chooses (nearest, top rated, newest).
  4. Tapping Call or WhatsApp records a lead for the vendor (they get a push notification), then opens the dialer or WhatsApp.
  5. A customer who contacted a vendor in the last 30 days can leave one review per contact; the vendor can reply.

2.4 Paid promotions and the Feed

Admin creates packages→Vendor buys one→Photo/video + caption + areas→Pay→Top of customers' Feed

The customer app's Feed shows, for the customer's area: first the live paid promotions targeting that area (marked "Sponsored", with Call / WhatsApp / View profile buttons), then the work photos and videos of vendors serving that area. Customers can like and comment on any post. Promotions go live on payment without review; an admin can hide one at any time.

2.5 Renewals and expiry

Every night the server checks subscriptions. A subscription past its end date enters a short grace period (still visible, with reminders), then expires and the vendor drops out of search. Vendors get reminders 15, 7 and 1 day before expiry and can renew from the app.

7. Super-admin panel — /super-admin

For super-admins only, with the same login but an extra two-step sign-in (a 6-digit code from an authenticator app, asked once per session and again after 30 idle minutes; recovery codes are provided). Blue accent so it is never confused with /admin.

ScreenWhat it's for

8. Key rules and concepts

Zones and sub-zones

The city is divided into main zones (e.g. Ahmedabad) and sub-zones (e.g. Satellite, Ranip). A customer is matched to the smallest active zone that contains their location — a main zone that has sub-zones never matches by itself. A location outside every active sub-zone matches nothing, and the customer sees no vendors. Draft (inactive) zones are ignored.

Plans, quotas and priority

Each plan sets how many categories, services, zones, photos and videos a vendor may have, and a priority rank that puts higher plans first in search. Vendors can buy add-ons (extra categories, zones, etc.) without changing plan.

Subscription life cycle

Waiting for payment→Active→Grace→Expired

Only Active and Grace subscriptions are visible to customers. Price, dates and quotas are always calculated on the server. Every subscription purchase carries a unique key so a double tap can never charge twice.

Account approval and KYC

Every self-registered account must be approved by an admin before it can use the product. A vendor can only be approved once every required KYC document is Verified — whether they signed up themselves, were added by a salesman or created by an admin. Documents with an expiry date expire automatically (nightly check) and the vendor is notified.

Passwords

Only email + password sign-in. New accounts set their first password from an emailed link. Passwords are only ever reset by the user themselves (from "Forgot password?") — admins never reset passwords. These links are the only emails the system sends.

Payments

  • Online: with "online payments via Razorpay" switched on, a payment link is sent by SMS and the purchase waits as "pending payment" until Razorpay confirms it. Switched off, online payments are recorded straight away (placeholder mode).
  • Cash: collected by a salesman, confirmed by an admin in Cash Reconciliation.
  • The apps never take payment themselves (keeps app-store billing rules out of it). All money is stored in paise.

Leads, calls and reviews

A lead is recorded every time a customer taps Call or WhatsApp — before the dialer opens. On Android the vendor app can confirm whether calls were answered and the talk time (it records only times, never phone numbers). Reviews are allowed only for a contact in the last 30 days, one review per contact, editable for 24 hours.

Paid promotions

Vendors can only target their own subscription areas, up to the package's limit. The days count from payment. No review before going live; admins can hide a promotion (the vendor is told why). A daily job ends expired promotions and reminds vendors the day before.

Notifications and live updates

Push notifications (Firebase) for new leads, approvals, document requests, expiry reminders, review requests and promotions. A live connection (Laravel Reverb) updates the app instantly when an account is approved or a document's status changes.

Setup points to watch
  • Sub-zones: only 3 areas are active today (Ambawadi, sector -2, ES ZONE) and most Ahmedabad sub-zones don't cover the city — customers outside them see no vendors. Redraw and activate sub-zones in the admin.
  • Places search key must be a server key (not restricted to Android apps) — set it in Super Admin → System settings → Maps.
  • Android Maps key must list the app's package name and SHA-1 fingerprint for each PC/keystore that builds the app.
  • Email: local development writes emails to the log file; set real mail settings in backend/.env to send them.
